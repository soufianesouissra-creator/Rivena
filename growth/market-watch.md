# Morocco market watch (as of 2026-11-15)

> Round 1 (2026-11-14, 6 facts kept) + round 2 (2026-11-15, 70 claims checked, all confirmed or corrected). Only checked facts are stated here. Open items are in section 7. RIVENA's own price ideas are not in this file while the repo is public.

## 1. Five things to know

- **The FMC150 (our tracker) is not end of life.** It is not on Teltonika's official EOL list, checked 2026-11-15 ([EOL list](https://www.teltonika-gps.com/support/eol-products)). Teltonika's wiki files it under "CAN Trackers & Adapters", not "EOL Products", and its datasheet was updated on 2026-11-12 ([wiki](https://wiki.teltonika-gps.com/view/FMC150); [wiki EOL category](https://wiki.teltonika-gps.com/view/Category:EOL_Products)). The round 1 claim "End of life, replaced by FMx250" is **refuted**. The "End of life" block on the product page is a template hidden by CSS, and its dates read "-". "FMx250" does not appear on the page at all ([FMC150 page](https://www.teltonika-gps.com/products/trackers/can-data/fmc150)). On a real EOL product page the same block is visible and has dates ([FMB230 page](https://www.teltonika-gps.com/products/trackers/advanced/fmb230)).
- **The FMC150 is changing inside.** From November 2026, new units ship with base firmware 04.02.05.Rev.05 (was 04.00.00.Rev.13), to support a new flash memory chip. Teltonika gives "product supply continuity" as the reason. These units cannot be downgraded to older firmware that does not support the new chip. During the switch, units may arrive with either version. From December 2026, all FM devices in production except the FMx64 family get the new flash chip. CE and E-mark certificates and purchase codes stay the same ([change notices](https://wiki.teltonika-gps.com/view/FMC150_Product_Change_Notifications)).
- **GPS in employee vehicles must be declared to the CNDP.** Under Délibération 17-2014, the employer files form F-214. It may keep the data for one year at most and track only during work use. It needs the driver's prior consent (or a legal exemption), must tell staff representatives by letter before installing, and must give drivers a notice that carries the CNDP receipt number ([deliberation PDF](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/deliberation-n-17-2014-10-01-2014.pdf); [CNDP procedure, July 2025](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/CNDP_Procedure-de-Notification-des-traitements_20250724.pdf)). Running a file without the required declaration carries a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 DH ([Law 09-08, art. 52](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Loi-09-08-Fr.pdf)).
- **Two local fleet competitors now have checked prices.** 7DAYS Techno (Casablanca, Navixy platform) starts at 99 MAD HT per vehicle per month for platform plus data SIM, hardware not included ([7DAYS demo page](https://7days-techno.com/demo-plateforme-suivi-gps/)). Mia Fleet (Kénitra) sells one-off turnkey packs at 999 to 2,499 DH TTC, installation included, with no recurring fee stated ([Mia Fleet](https://mia-fleet.com/)). Details are in section 2.
- **E-invoicing still has no start date.** The DGI announced a rollout "during 2026". It uses a clearance model: each invoice is checked and validated on the tax platform before it reaches the client. B2B comes first. The decree that will set the calendar was still a draft at the SGG ([Le Matin, 6 Oct 2026](https://lematin.ma/economie/facturation-electronique-un-nouveau-cap-pour-les-entreprises-marocaines/369166)). A keyword search of the Bulletin officiel (Arabic edition, n° 7500 to 7559, 16 Apr to 9 Nov 2026, with n° 7557 missing) found no e-invoicing decree ([BO 7559](https://www.sgg.gov.ma/BO/AR/3111/2026/BO_7559_Ar.pdf)).

**CNDP rules in Délibération 17-2014** (what a fleet client must do) ([PDF](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/deliberation-n-17-2014-10-01-2014.pdf)):
- **Allowed purposes:** fleet management. Help for the employee after an incident or accident. Security of goods and vehicles, notably against theft. Driver performance, only if no other means exists. Billing a service directly tied to the vehicle's use.
- **When:** tracking may run only while the vehicle is used for work.
- **Data allowed:** name, first name, work contact details, plate number, position, km driven, times and durations of use, driving time, number of stops, average speed.
- **Retention:** one year at most.
- **Notice to vehicle users, 7 items:** controller's name; that the vehicle has GPS; data collected; recipients; purpose; contact for access, correction and objection; CNDP receipt number.
- **Consent:** prior, free and informed, or a justified exemption under Law 09-08.
- **Staff representatives:** told by letter, within a reasonable time, before installation.
- **Access:** limited to the fleet manager and, where relevant, HR.
- **Transfer abroad:** the CNDP must be notified first.
- **Linking with other files:** combining GPS data with files that have different main purposes (for example ERP or HR files used for other ends) needs a separate authorisation.
- No sign was found that a later text replaced this deliberation, but that was not fully checked.

**E-invoicing: what is known so far** (vendor sources, not DGI texts):
- Each invoice becomes a structured UBL file with a qualified e-signature, validated in real time by the tax administration. This is Sage's summary of what the DGI head said in May 2026 ([Sage](https://www.sage.com/fr-ma/blog/facturation-electronique-maroc-2026/)).
- Two channels are planned: a free DGI web portal at fatourati.gov.ma for very small firms, and an EDI link for firms with an ERP. Certified service providers (CSPs) may also validate invoices later. At launch the DGI does it ([Sage](https://www.sage.com/fr-ma/blog/facturation-electronique-maroc-2026/)).
- A PDF sent by email will not count as an e-invoice. A firm will need connected management software or the administration's portal. This is a Sage executive's reading ([Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/economie/facturation-electronique-un-nouveau-cap-pour-les-entreprises-marocaines/369166)).
- No size thresholds or dates per category are published ([Sage](https://www.sage.com/fr-ma/blog/facturation-electronique-maroc-2026/)).
